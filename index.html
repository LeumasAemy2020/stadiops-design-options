Recommendation

For a site that looks launched and established, use Rinkside as the global system.

Rinkside is the closest to how proven B2B SaaS sites are built: real customers in real places, a clean product-first page, and orange kept for actions. It is also the simplest to build in Breakdance. If you want more brand personality, add the Team Kit sash to the final CTA only. Two things to settle first: photo consent from families, and photos from sports beyond hockey so the site does not read as hockey-only. If photos cannot be cleared in time, launch with Bento, which needs only product screenshots, and move to Rinkside later. Both use the same white and grey sections, so the switch only touches the hero and CTA.

Build it in Breakdance
